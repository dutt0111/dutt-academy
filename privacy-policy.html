DUTT ACADEMY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 04/09/2026

Dutt Academy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, why we collect it, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • Name
  • Parent/Guardian name, where provided
  • Phone number
  • Class or course
  • Message or enquiry details

We do not ask you to provide unnecessary personal information through the enquiry form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through the enquiry form to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Contact you regarding courses and batches
  • Provide information about fees and admissions
  • Respond to questions about Dutt Academy programmes

We do not use the information for purposes unrelated to the enquiry unless permitted by applicable law or you provide appropriate consent.

3. Consent

Before submitting the enquiry form, you are asked to provide affirmative consent for Dutt Academy to collect and use the information you provide for the purposes described above.

You may contact Dutt Academy regarding information you have provided.

4. Sharing of Information

Information submitted through the enquiry form is used by Dutt Academy for responding to enquiries and providing information about courses, batches, fees and admissions. We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessed by authorised personnel of Dutt Academy where necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide related services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information submitted through the enquiry form only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide information about courses and admissions, and maintain necessary records. When the information is no longer required for these purposes, it will be securely deleted or anonymised, unless retention is required by law.

7. Your Requests

You may contact Dutt Academy to ask about personal information submitted through the website or to request appropriate correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements.

8. Children's Information

Because Dutt Academy provides educational services, enquiries may relate to students who are children.

Parents or lawful guardians should provide information on behalf of a child where required by applicable law.

Dutt Academy does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children through the website.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services, or information-handling practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
